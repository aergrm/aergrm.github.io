  <div class="portfolio-card">
    <h3>Hawkish Leaders and Foreign Policy</h3>
    <div class="portfolio-meta">Journal article · <em>International Political Science Review</em> · 2025</div>
    <p>
      This article tests whether leaders commonly described as “hawkish” actually share the political and strategic beliefs that the label implies. Using large-scale speech data and operational code analysis, it shows that hawkishness is more heterogeneous than conventional labels suggest.
    </p>

    <h3>Semiconductor Research Trends of Middle Powers</h3>
    <div class="portfolio-meta">Book chapter · Turkish Academy of Sciences · 2024</div>
    <p>
      This chapter examines how Japan, South Korea, Türkiye, and Malaysia participate in semiconductor research, patenting, and collaboration networks. It compares established and emerging middle powers in a strategic technology sector increasingly shaped by geopolitical competition.
    </p>
