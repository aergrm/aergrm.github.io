### About the book

The book examines Australia, Canada, the Netherlands, South Korea, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, India, and Brazil. The comparative framework explains how these governments combine domestic investment with foreign purchases and partnerships, choose between broad and specialized AI ambitions, and seek influence amid U.S.–China technology competition. It also examines the political and institutional conditions shaping their autonomy.
