Methods and sources

Cooperation

The cooperation layer contains official bilateral agreements and selected multilateral frameworks. Each record includes participating countries, year, type, status, description, and a source link. An agreement documents a formal commitment; it does not by itself establish implementation, investment, or production.

Firm-network findings

The research-findings section summarizes Köse et al. (2026), which reconstructs supply, partnership, and ownership relationships from company webpages archived through 2025. The website reports only findings presented in the paper. It does not assign national scores or recreate the authors’ firm-level network because the full derived edge list is not publicly downloadable.

Source: Köse et al. (2026).

Trade

The trade layer uses CEPII BACI HS22 version 202601 for 2024. It includes HS 8542 integrated circuits, HS 8486 semiconductor-manufacturing equipment, HS 381800 doped electronic-grade materials, and HS 8541 semiconductor devices excluding photovoltaic cells and modules under 854142 and 854143.

The country indicators report imports, exports, partner concentration, top-partner shares, and import shares from selected major sources. Customs data do not measure design licensing, software, services, ownership, contract terms, or export-control authorization.

Source: CEPII BACI.

Country profiles

Capabilities, dependencies, and value-chain roles are descriptive research codings. They are presented separately from measured trade indicators. The page does not provide policy recommendations to the countries.
