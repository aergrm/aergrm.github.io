Methods and data documentation

Middle Powers in the Global Semiconductor Network

Release 1.1: July 2026. Trade indicators use a fixed 2024 release and do not query external APIs when a visitor opens the page.

1. Research objective

The project asks how technologically consequential middle powers use specialized capabilities and international cooperation to manage semiconductor dependence. It combines qualitative value-chain roles and official cooperation records with reproducible bilateral trade, concentration, and partner-exposure measures.

2. Country selection

The sample contains established semiconductor powers, emerging manufacturing hubs, design and research centers, resource suppliers, and capital-rich entrants. “Middle power” is an analytical project category rather than a formal legal classification. The United States, China, and the European Union are displayed as external anchors.

3. Fixed trade data

Trade measures are constructed from CEPII BACI HS22 version 202601, using flows recorded in 2024. BACI reconciles exporter and importer reports from UN Comtrade, adjusts reported import values toward an FOB basis, and weights reports using country reliability. Values in the source files are thousands of current U.S. dollars and are converted to dollars in the processing script.

The 2024 BACI release is the latest common bilateral year available in version 202601. CEPII cautions that the most recent year may be revised in later releases. The website therefore identifies the precise source version rather than calling the data “live.”

4. Semiconductor trade basket

Product groupHS22 codesInterpretation
Integrated circuits8542Electronic integrated circuits and related components.
Non-photovoltaic semiconductor devices8541, excluding 854142 and 854143Diodes, transistors, LEDs, transducers, and other semiconductor devices. Photovoltaic cells and assembled modules are excluded to prevent solar-energy trade from dominating the chip-network measures.
Manufacturing equipment8486Machinery and apparatus used to manufacture semiconductor boules, wafers, devices, or integrated circuits, plus covered parts and accessories.
Electronic-grade wafers and materials381800Doped chemical elements and compounds in forms used in electronics, including discs and wafers.

The basket is intentionally transparent and reproducible. It is broader than integrated circuits alone but does not capture design services, IP licensing, software, ownership, contract terms, subsidies, or the technical sophistication of individual shipments.

5. Country trade indicators

Imports and exports are sums of all positive bilateral BACI flows in the four product groups. The trade balance is exports minus imports. The export/import ratio is basket exports divided by basket imports.

Partner concentration is measured with the Herfindahl–Hirschman index, HHI = Σs², where s is each partner’s share of imports or exports. The index approaches one when trade is concentrated in a single partner and falls as the partner portfolio becomes more dispersed. The “effective number of partners” is 1 / HHI. Top-one and top-three shares provide directly interpretable companion measures.

6. Measured anchor exposure

The country profiles report the share of basket imports originating in the United States, China, Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, and the EU-27. These are observed bilateral trade shares—not ordinal expert scores. EU exposure sums flows from all EU-27 members; for the Netherlands and the EU aggregate, internal/self flows are excluded.

BACI and UN Comtrade do not publish Taiwan under its own name. Following CEPII guidance, the project uses country code 490, “Asia, not elsewhere specified,” as a Taiwan proxy and labels it explicitly throughout the release.

7. Bilateral trade network

Solid directional links show exporter-to-importer flows among mapped countries and anchors. Link width uses a logarithmic transformation of bilateral value to preserve visibility across a highly skewed distribution. Link color identifies the largest product group in that bilateral flow. In overview mode, the map limits the display to the largest flows; selecting a country displays its principal mapped relationships.

8. Cooperation records and value-chain roles

Bilateral cooperation records are included when an official government source identifies a semiconductor partnership, alliance, memorandum, or formal framework. Multilateral entries are coded as membership records. Every record in the public cooperation file includes a source URL.

Value-chain roles and the accompanying country narratives remain qualitative research codings. They provide substantive interpretation that customs data cannot supply. The earlier zero-to-five exposure bars and qualitative dependence corridors are no longer used as quantitative evidence.

9. Reproducibility files

Country indicators CSV Bilateral flows CSV Python processing script Website data file

The Python script downloads a single versioned BACI archive, filters the 2024 HS22 file, calculates the indicators, and writes the website data and CSV outputs. The retained GitHub workflow is manual and reproducibility-oriented; the public page itself contains no live trade request.
