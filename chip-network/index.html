Research question

How do selected middle powers use specialized capabilities and international cooperation to manage dependence in the semiconductor value chain?

The page showsOfficial cooperation records, implementation status, bilateral customs trade, country profiles, published firm-network findings, and curated firm and institution profiles for India, Malaysia, and Canada.
The page does not showA complete firm census, private supplier relationships, technological sophistication, full production capacity, causal effects, or implementation not supported by documented evidence.
1

No selected case reproduces the full chain

Domestic strengths remain concentrated in particular stages, while equipment, intellectual property, fabrication scale, materials, or markets remain externally controlled.

2

Inherited capabilities shape cooperation

India seeks broad capability access, Malaysia protects and upgrades an embedded manufacturing role, and Canada builds specialized research, sensor, photonics, and packaging niches.

3

Implementation matters more than signatures

Agreements are coded separately as announced, signed, funded, open call, under implementation, or operational rather than treated as equivalent outcomes.

How to read the evidence layers

Cooperation

Documents governmental arrangements and their stated scope, stage, and implementation status. A record is not evidence that a capability was successfully created.

Trade

Measures 2024 bilateral customs flows in four product groups. It does not observe ownership, licensing, design software, services, or process sophistication.

Capability profiles

Describe representative firms, facilities, research institutions, territorial production, and external dependencies in India, Malaysia, and Canada.

Published network research

Summarizes findings from a separate study of firm relationships through 2025. The underlying firm-level edge list is not reconstructed here.

Why this matters

Dependence is also a resilience and security problem

Semiconductor partnerships affect commercial continuity, trusted access, critical infrastructure, and the ability to sustain defense-relevant production during disruption. The site distinguishes visible access arrangements from demonstrated capability.
